# Controls

The default controls for keyboard and mouse, Xbox and PlayStation controllers, and how to change
them.

## Default controls

| Action | Keyboard & mouse | Xbox | PlayStation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move | W A S D | Left stick | Left stick |
| Look (camera) | Mouse | Right stick | Right stick |
| Light attack | Left mouse | RB | R1 |
| Heavy attack (lunge) | Right mouse | RT | R2 |
| Roll (dodge) | Ctrl, or Cmd on a Mac | B | Circle |
| Sprint (hold) | Shift | LT | L2 |
| Jump (towards low cover: [vault](combat.md#sprint-jump-and-stamina)) | Space | A | Cross |
| Ability | E | Y | Triangle |
| Scoreboard (hold) | Tab | View | Create |
| Pause menu | Esc | Menu | Options |

Your character faces the way you move. When you attack or use your ability, it turns to where
the camera is looking, so you always strike where you aim.

## Controllers

Plug in or pair an Xbox or PlayStation controller and press **any button**: the game switches
to it, and the menus, the settings and your ability tile show that controller's buttons. Press
any key or click the mouse to switch back. Other controllers work too and show Xbox buttons.

![Settings → Controls on an Xbox controller](images/controls-controller.jpg)

## Changing your controls

Open **Settings → Controls**. The page shows the device you're using right now, so you rebind
the keyboard with the keyboard and the controller with the controller.

1. Click (or select) the binding you want to change.
2. Press the new key, mouse button or controller button. Esc (or Start/Options) cancels.
3. If that input was already used by another action, the two swap, so nothing is left
   unbound.

**Reset to defaults** puts back the defaults for the device shown. On a controller, moving and
the camera always stay on the sticks.

![Settings → Controls on the keyboard](images/controls-keyboard.jpg)

## Menus without a mouse

- **Keyboard:** press an arrow key to start. The arrows move between buttons, **Enter**
  presses, **Esc** goes back. Clicking with the mouse ends it.
- **Controller:** the D-pad or left stick moves, **A / Cross** presses, **B / Circle** goes
  back. On long pages, keep pressing down to scroll.
- In this wiki, **PageUp / PageDown** or the **right stick** scroll the page.
