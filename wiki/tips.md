# Tips

Habits that win more fights. Read [Combat](combat.md) first.

## Fighting

- **Don't swing first at the same time.** The light attack is fast; the heavy's wind-up is easy
  to read. Wait for the other player's heavy, roll through it, then punish the 0.6 s recovery.
- **Circle behind.** A heavy from behind is an [execute](combat.md#execute): an instant kill.
- **Watch the tell.** Zain's leap is the only warning before a
  [Drop Kick](abilities.md#zain-drop-kick). Roll sideways or through it.
- **Light, then kick.** A light hit (35) plus a Drop Kick (70) kills from full health.

## Stamina

- **Keep 30 for a roll.** Sprinting everywhere leaves you unable to dodge when it matters.
- If the bar turns amber, back off: you can't sprint away until it's refilled to 15.

## Staying alive

- **Break off after a hit.** Three seconds without being hit and you start
  [healing](combat.md#healing) one point a second.
- Use the [arena's](arena.md) cover: the crate yard and barrier maze are good places to escape.
- **Vault instead of going around.** Run at a barrier or crate and jump: you go over it, and
  whoever is chasing you has to follow or lose you.

## Scoring

- **Hunt the bounty.** A player on a streak is worth 1 + their bounty. The announcements tell
  you who.
- **Plan for the Golden Knife.** It spawns 45 s in and 30 s after it's lost. Be near the plaza
  when it's due, and when someone else has it, keep away from the gold light or roll their
  hits.
