# The arena

The only map for now: a walled 60 × 60 m yard floating above the clouds, with the Golden Knife
in the middle and a different kind of cover in each corner.

![The arena from above, with its zones](images/arena-map.jpg)

## Zones

| Zone | Where | What's there |
|---|---|---|
| Plaza | Centre | The Golden Knife plinth, a ring of low barriers, street lamps |
| Avenues | Four lanes from the plaza to the walls | Open ground with a few barriers |
| Crate yard | One corner | Shipping containers and stacked crates making tight alleys |
| Barrier maze | One corner | Rows of concrete barriers with gaps, a container at the back |
| Boulder field | One corner | Big rocks and an L-shaped wall |
| Depot | One corner | A raised metal deck with two ramps, barrels |
| Spawn lane | Along all four walls | Clear ground where everyone spawns, facing the middle |

### Plaza

The busiest spot: the [Golden Knife](match-rules.md#the-golden-knife) appears on the plinth
here. The barrier ring gives just enough cover to break a chase, and you can
[vault](combat.md#sprint-jump-and-stamina) straight over it.

### Crate yard and barrier maze

Tight corners where a [heavy lunge](combat.md#heavy-attack) is hard to see coming and a
[roll](combat.md#the-roll-dodge) can take you out of sight.

### Boulder field

Scattered rocks to circle around. Good for losing someone who's chasing you.

### Depot

The deck is the only high ground. The ramps are the way up, and jumping off the edge is the
fast way down.
