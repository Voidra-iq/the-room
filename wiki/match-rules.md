# Match rules

How a match is won: the modes, how kills are scored, bounties, the Golden Knife, Last Call and
the results screen.

## Modes

| Mode | Players | Score to win |
|---|---|---|
| Duel Pit | 6 to 10 | 25 points |
| Chaos | 12 to 20 | 40 points |

A match also ends after **8 minutes**. Then the player with the most points wins.

## Scoring

A kill is worth **1 point plus the victim's bounty**. Holding the Golden Knife doubles it.

### Bounty

Every kill raises **your** bounty by 1; dying resets it to 0. Killing someone with a high
bounty is worth a lot, so a player on a streak becomes everyone's target. Streaks are
announced to the whole room:

| Kills in a row | Announcement |
|---|---|
| 3 | NAME IS ON A TEAR |
| 5 | NAME IS UNSTOPPABLE (5) |
| 8 | NAME IS UNSTOPPABLE (8) |

Ending a streak is announced too: "NAME COLLECTED NAME'S BOUNTY".

## The Golden Knife

- It appears on the plinth in the middle of the [plaza](arena.md#plaza) **45 seconds** after
  the match starts: a gold knife, spinning and glowing above the plinth. The whole room is told
  when it does.
- Walk onto the plinth to take it. Everyone is told who took it, and a **column of gold light**
  rises from the holder into the sky, so the whole arena can see where they are. The holder
  fights with the gold knife in their hand.
- For **20 seconds**, every hit the holder lands kills in one blow, and every kill scores
  **double**.
- The holder loses it when they die or the 20 seconds run out. It comes back **30 seconds**
  later.

![The gold light marks the Golden Knife holder](images/golden-knife.jpg)

## Last Call

Last Call starts when the leader reaches **75%** of the score target or when **90 seconds** are
left. It's announced, the clock turns red, and respawns get faster (1 s), so the ending is
always a scramble.

In the last 5 seconds a big red **5, 4, 3, 2, 1** counts down in the middle of the screen.

![The final countdown](images/countdown.jpg)

## Results and awards

When the match ends, the results screen shows for 8 seconds, then the next match starts by
itself. The top three stand on the podium: **gold** for the winner (the MVP), **silver** for
second, **bronze** for third. Everyone else is listed above them.

![The results screen and its podium](images/results.jpg)

Awards go to the standouts of the match:

| Award | Goes to |
|---|---|
| Untouchable | the most hits dodged with the roll |
| Highway Robbery | the most points from a single bounty |
| All Bark, No Bite | the most heavy attacks that missed |
| Most Stabbed in the Back | the most times executed |
